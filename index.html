How to Play 17-0

Build the ultimate NFL all-time team and see if you can go 17-0!

The Draft

Each round, spin to get a random NFL team and era.
Select one player from that combination to add to your roster.
Don't like your spin? Use a Swap to re-roll the team or era (2 per game).

Building Your Lineup

Fill all 8 positions: QB RB WR WR K EDGE LB DB
Tap a player to select, then choose their roster slot.

Team Rating

Each player has an overall rating based on their peak season performance.
Higher combined ratings win more games.
Aim for the perfect 17-0 season!
